About me

I am a third-year undergraduate studying Mathematics and Applied Mathematics at the CSU–Dundee International College, Central South University, in a dual-degree program with the University of Dundee. I expect to graduate in June 2028.

My research focuses on efficient long-context inference for large language models, particularly learnable sparse attention. My sole-authored work, LSSA, explores how denoised supervision can guide the selection of useful attention dependencies.

I am also interested in geometric representation learning, including hyperbolic embeddings, and its potential applications to medical image segmentation and visual question answering.

Research

MANUSCRIPT UNDER REVIEWAAAI 2027 · Phase 2

LSSA: Learnable Sparse Semantic Attention for Efficient Long-Context Prefilling

Yishu Wu · Sole author · Oct. 2025–Jun. 2026

How can a language model select useful attention connections without first computing dense attention? LSSA learns block-sparse attention patterns from denoised supervision, separating offline target construction from efficient online inference.

Evaluated on four model families using RULER, with context lengths up to 128K tokens.
