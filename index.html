About me

I am a third-year undergraduate studying Mathematics and Applied Mathematics at the CSU–Dundee International College, Central South University (Project 985), in a dual-degree program with the University of Dundee. Graduation is expected in June 2028.

My research interests include large language models, computer vision, and geometric representation learning. My current work focuses on efficient long-context inference, particularly learnable sparse attention. LSSA explores how denoised supervision can guide the selection of useful attention dependencies.

I am also interested in exploring hyperbolic embeddings for medical image segmentation and visual question answering.

Research

MANUSCRIPT UNDER REVIEWAAAI 2027 · Phase 2

LSSA: Learnable Sparse Semantic Attention for Efficient Long-Context Prefilling

Yishu Wu · First author (sole author)

How can a language model select useful attention connections without first computing dense attention? LSSA learns block-sparse attention patterns from denoised supervision, separating offline target construction from efficient online inference.

Evaluated on four model families using RULER, with context lengths up to 128K tokens.
